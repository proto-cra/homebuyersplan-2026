How the HBP works

The Home Buyers' Plan (HBP) is a Canada Revenue Agency program that lets you to make a withdrawal from your registered retirement savings plans (RRSPs) to buy or build a qualifying home for yourself or for a specified disabled person.

The maximum you can withdraw is $60,000.

Withdraw from your RRSP tax-free

HBP withdrawals are tax-free. Your RRSP issuer will not withhold tax on amounts you withdraw of $60,000 or less.

You can make your withdrawal from more than one RRSP as long as you are the annuitant of each RRSP account. Your spouse or common-law-partner can also withdraw up to $60,000 from their RRSP.

Some RRSPs, such as locked-in or group RRSPs, do not allow you to make an HBP withdrawal from them.

For more information: How to participate in the HBP

Repay your withdrawal over 15 years

You have up to 15 years to pay back the withdrawals you made from your RRSP under the HBP.

You have the option to repay the amounts to your RRSP, pooled registered pension plan (PRPP), or specified pension plan (SPP).

Your repayment period will start 2 to 5 years after you made your first withdrawal, depending on what year you started participating in the HBP.
